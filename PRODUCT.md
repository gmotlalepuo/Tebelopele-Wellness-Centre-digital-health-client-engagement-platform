# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are people in Botswana seeking Tebelopele services, reliable health information, appointments, referrals or human support, often from a phone and sometimes on an unreliable connection. Staff users include reception and appointment officers, health workers, support agents, content editors and reviewers, referral officers, reporting users, administrators and auditors.

## Product Purpose

Provide one trustworthy digital route into Tebelopele services. Clients should be able to find approved information, manage permitted service journeys and move from automated guidance to an appropriate human without losing context. Staff should be able to operate those journeys through permission-controlled workspaces with clear audit history.

## Positioning

Tebelopele brings approved health information, service access and accountable human assistance into one continuous client conversation.

## Brand Personality

Reassuring, capable and human. The interface should feel recognizably Tebelopele and locally grounded, using direct language and calm, respectful guidance.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI imagery, purple-blue technology gradients, decorative glass surfaces, fake operational metrics, oversized dashboard decoration, excessive cards, clinical stock-photo clichés and interfaces that make the model more prominent than the client's immediate need.

## Design Principles

- Put the next useful action before system explanation.
- Make trust visible through approved sources, clear status and accountable human ownership.
- Preserve continuity across public pages, authenticated journeys, chat and WhatsApp.
- Work well on small screens and variable connections before adding visual flourish.
- Use Tebelopele's sunrise and plum identity with restraint and purpose.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard and screen-reader use, visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, clear validation and touch targets suitable for mobile use. Design copy and content structures for future multilingual delivery.
